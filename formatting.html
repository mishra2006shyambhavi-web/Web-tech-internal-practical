Formatting Styles and Headings

Text Formatting Tags

Use: <b>/<strong> to make text bold.

Use: <i>/<em> to make text italic.

Use: <u> to underline text.

Use: <strike>/<s> to show strikethrough text.

Use: <sub> for subscript, e.g. H2O.

Use: <sup> for superscript, e.g. x2 or E = mc2.
